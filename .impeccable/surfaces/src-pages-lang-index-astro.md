## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio as an author's edition whose cover is its table of contents: name on one side, the work indexed with page numbers on the other. It refuses the hero-plus-card-grid developer page and the stacked sections of equal weight.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground #111110, warm paper ink #EFEBE2, muted #9A968C, hairline rules #2A2A27, a single volt accent #E8FF5A reserved for "llevadas a producción", the hovered entry and selection. Noto Serif Display for the name, index entries and the lede, with italics for page numbers and emphasis. Pixelify Sans for uppercase meta labels, Hanken Grotesk for reading text. Pill ES/EN switch, outlined pill CTA, dotted leaders.
